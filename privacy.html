Privacy policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

Data controller: Xentraffic, operator of monsterbet.ai. Privacy contact: contact@monsterbet.ai.

This site has no account, no login, no newsletter and no contact form. We never ask you for your name, email address or payment details, and there is nowhere on this site to give them to us.

1. What we collect

Only what is needed to measure whether our advertising works:

  • Analytics events — pages viewed, and clicks on subscription buttons, together with which button and which section of the page it sat in.
  • Campaign parameters from the link you arrived on — the advertising source, medium, campaign, ad and keyword, and the Google click identifier (gclid, gbraid or wbraid) when you arrive from a Google ad. These identify the advertisement, not you.
  • Technical data your browser sends — approximate location (country/region level), device type, browser, and the site you came from.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, address or payment information. We do not sell data, and we do not build advertising audiences or profiles from visitors to this site.

3. Cookies

This site itself sets no cookies. Google Analytics sets its own cookies to recognise a returning visit and to attribute a session to a campaign. Blocking them in your browser, or using an ad blocker, prevents our analytics from recording your visit and does not stop you using this site or subscribing.

Whop sets an affiliate cookie on its own domain when you arrive at its checkout from our link. That cookie is how Monster.bet knows to credit the referral to us. It is set by Whop, not by us, and is governed by Whop's policy.

4. Why we are allowed to do this

Where the UK and EU GDPR apply, our lawful basis is your consent for analytics cookies, and our legitimate interest in understanding whether our advertising reaches real people for the campaign measurement itself. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing and blocking cookies in your browser.

5. How long it is kept

Analytics data is retained by Google Analytics under the retention setting on the property — a maximum of 14 months for event-level data — and is then deleted automatically. Server logs are kept by GitHub under its own schedule.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export data about you, to object to processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority.

A practical note, offered honestly: because this site collects no identifiers we can tie to a person, we are usually unable to find "your" data in our analytics in order to act on such a request — there is no name, email or account to search on. If you want a request actioned anyway, write to contact@monsterbet.ai and we will do what we can, and tell you plainly where we cannot. For anything relating to your Monster.bet subscription, the controller is Monster.bet, not us.

7. Children

This site is for adults aged 18 or over — 21 or over in United States jurisdictions where that is the legal age for sports wagering. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
