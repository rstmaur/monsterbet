Week 1 research capabilities

Matchup, player and market research

Three research surfaces you can run against any Week 1 football game. Every answer is reasoning you can interrogate — never a pick, a guarantee or a wager.

Matchup research

Break a football game into the parts that decide it: tempo, personnel groupings, pressure looks, coverage tendencies and the situational splits that actually repeat.

  • How two schemes collide
  • Which unit decides the game
  • Situational and pace splits
  • Follow-ups until it holds up

Injury & player research

Read past the designation. Trace who absorbs the snaps, how a role is really built, what changes when the depth chart moves, and what the status tag does not tell you.

  • Snap and usage dependencies
  • Role shape, not headline status
  • Depth-chart knock-on effects
  • What still needs verifying

Market-movement research

Frame what a move means before you trust it. Compare where a number started against where it sits, separate news from flow, and decide which explanation survives scrutiny.

  • Opened versus current
  • News-driven or flow-driven
  • Book-to-book disagreement
  • What the move does not prove
